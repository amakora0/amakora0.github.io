Hello! I have a PhD in computer science from the [Department of Computer Science](https://www.cs.jhu.edu/) at [Johns Hopkins University](https://www.jhu.edu/), where I was advised by [Michael Dinitz](https://www.cs.jhu.edu/~mdinitz/). My research was in theoretical computer science, namely in graph and approximation algorithms. I was supported by the [NSF Graduate Research Fellowship](https://www.nsfgrfp.org/). Before starting my PhD, I earned a BS in Mathematics from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology.

In November, I will be joining the Applied Math group at Boeing.
