Hello! I'm an Applied Mathematician in the [Boeing Applied Math](https://www.siam.org/publications/siam-news/articles/spotlight-on-the-boeing-math-group/) group. 

I have a PhD in Computer Science from the [Department of Computer Science](https://www.cs.jhu.edu/) at [Johns Hopkins University](https://www.jhu.edu/), where I was advised by [Michael Dinitz](https://www.cs.jhu.edu/~mdinitz/). My research was in discrete mathematics and theoretical computer science, particularly in graph algorithms, approximation algorithms, network design, and combinatorial optimization. I was supported in part by the [NSF Graduate Research Fellowship](https://www.nsfgrfp.org/). Before my PhD, I earned a BS in Mathematics from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT).
